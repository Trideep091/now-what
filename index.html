THE PROBLEM

Boredom isn't a lack of time.
It's a lack of ideas.

People often have free time but don't know how to spend it meaningfully. They ask: "What should I do?" or "Bro, I'm bored. What can I do for the next 3 hours?"

🌀

The Doomscroll Trap

When you don't have a plan, you default to Instagram Reels, YouTube shorts, or refreshing group chats. Two hours vanish and you feel more drained than before.

🧭

Context Matters

Generic lists of "100 things to do" ignore that you're in a hostel room with ₹150, or that you're too exhausted for high-effort cooking. NowWhat? builds around your exact reality.

⚡

Sequential Flow

Instead of isolated suggestions, we structure a balanced sequence: start with a chai or stretch, transition into discovery, and wrap up with satisfaction.
